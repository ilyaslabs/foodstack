FoodStack observability is installing in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Grafana (dashboards in the FoodStack folder, logs and traces under Explore):
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "obs.name" . }}-grafana 3000:3000
  open http://localhost:3000 and sign in as admin with:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "obs.name" . }}-grafana -o jsonpath='{.data.admin-password}' | base64 -d

Send traces from FoodStack (its pods ship their logs already):
  helm upgrade <release> k8s/helm/foodstack -n <namespace> --reuse-values --set server.tracing.enabled=true
