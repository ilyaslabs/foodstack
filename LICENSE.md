## 2. Definitions

1. **"Software"** means the software distributed under this license, including modified versions and works based on it.
2. **"You"** or **"Licensee"** means the person or company that runs a business or product built using the Software, together with any company it controls or is controlled by.
3. **"Covered Business"** means the business, product, or service you run using the Software. If you also run activities that do not use the Software, only the Covered Business counts.
4. **"Licensee Revenue"** means all amounts you earn **in your own right** from the Covered Business, measured before your own costs and expenses. It includes, for example:
   - commissions, percentage or fixed, that you charge sellers, stores, or other third parties;
   - fees you charge end customers (service fees, platform fees, small-order fees, delivery fees that you keep);
   - subscription, listing, onboarding, or membership fees;
   - advertising, featured-placement, and promotion fees;
   - mark-ups or margins you keep on payment processing, currency conversion, or similar services;
   - **10%** of the price of goods or services that you yourself make or provide and sell through the Covered Business (see section 2.8).
5. **"Pass-Through Amounts"** are **not** Licensee Revenue. They are amounts you collect as an agent or intermediary and owe to someone else:
   - the price of goods or services sold by a third party (for example a store) that you collect and pass on to them, minus your commission;
   - delivery charges that belong to a third party who did the delivery;
   - tips, gratuities, and donations that go in full to another person (for example a rider or driver);
   - sales tax, VAT, GST, and similar taxes you collect and pay to a government;
   - refunds, chargebacks, and cancelled orders, to the extent the money is returned;
   - security deposits and other money held for someone else and returned to them.
6. **Discounts and bad debt.** Licensee Revenue is counted after discounts, coupons, and credits that **you** fund, and after amounts you invoiced but could not collect and have written off in good faith. Discounts funded by a third party do not reduce your Licensee Revenue.
7. **How money moves does not matter.** An amount is Licensee Revenue because you earned it, not because it went through your bank account. Commission owed to you by a seller who collected the payment directly, or cash collected on your behalf, counts. Money that only passed through your account and belongs to someone else does not.
8. **First-party sales.** When you sell your own goods or services directly to end customers through the Covered Business (for example a restaurant running the Software as its own ordering site), only 10% of those sales, after taxes, refunds and discounts you fund, count as Licensee Revenue. This keeps the effective share at 0.5% of such sales. Delivery fees, service fees and other charges you add on top still count in full. Goods bought from others and resold through the Covered Business are not first-party sales.

## 3. Revenue Sharing Terms

1. **Revenue Threshold**: You can use the Software for free until your Covered Business has earned **$10,000 USD in cumulative Licensee Revenue**.
2. **Contribution Obligation**: After that point, you agree to pay **5% of your monthly Licensee Revenue** to the original author. In the calendar month in which you cross the threshold, the 5% applies only to the Licensee Revenue above $10,000 USD; in every later month it applies to all of that month's Licensee Revenue.
3. **Currency**: Amounts in other currencies are converted to USD at a reasonable published exchange rate for the month concerned.
4. **Self-Reporting**: You are responsible for reporting your Licensee Revenue honestly. A simple monthly figure, with a breakdown by revenue type on request, is enough.
5. **Payment Arrangements**: After reaching the threshold, contact the author at **m.ilyas@live.com** to arrange payment details and invoicing. Each month's payment is due within 30 days after the end of that calendar month.
6. **Audit Clause (Trust-Based)**: This license relies on trust. The author may request, in good faith, basic validation of your reported Licensee Revenue (for example a summary from your accounting or ledger records).
